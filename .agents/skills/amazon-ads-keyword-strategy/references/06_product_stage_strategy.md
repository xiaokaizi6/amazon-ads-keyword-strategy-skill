# Product Stage Strategy

Evidence base: the data supports stage-dependent decisions, especially for new launch, growth/ranking, stable optimization, and seasonal windows. Some stage guidance is inferred from rules and conflicts, so mark unsupported specifics as `low confidence`.

## Stage Map

### 上架前

- Goal: prepare listing relevance, review plan, inventory timing, and keyword map.
- Ads: do not scale before listing can convert. Prepare core, long-tail, attribute, competitor, and ranking target lists.
- Data needed: target keywords, competitor set, expected CPC, expected CVR, margin, inventory.
- Risk: launching ads into a weak listing and later misreading poor CVR as keyword failure.

### 新品 0-14 天

- Goal: collect exposure, clicks, search-term data, early conversion signal.
- Ads: auto discovery, limited phrase/broad, small exact set for known terms, strict budget caps.
- Metrics: CTR, CPC, spend, first orders, search-term relevance, indexing/ranking visibility.
- Caveat: ACOS may be unstable; do not optimize as if the product is mature.

### 新品 15-45 天

- Goal: convert early data into structure and decide whether to push ranking.
- Ads: move proven terms to exact, isolate ranking targets, keep discovery budgets controlled, start competitor/ASIN tests only when offer strength is clear.
- Metrics: CVR, ACOS, TACOS, ad order share, natural rank, natural order share.
- Conflict: 放量 vs 控 ACOS must be decided by margin, budget, sample size, and ranking goal.

### 爬坡期

- Goal: expand orders and natural rank without hiding weak economics.
- Ads: dedicated rank-push exact campaigns, profitable exact campaigns, separate broad/phrase discovery, selective product targeting.
- Metrics: target-term ad rank, natural rank, total orders, TACOS, CVR versus competitor/category.
- Risk:中小词出单 does not automatically push大词 natural ranking.

### 稳定期

- Goal: profit, traffic structure, defense, and efficient ranking maintenance.
- Ads: exact profit terms, brand defense, selective ASIN defense/offense, low-noise discovery.
- Metrics: TACOS, natural order share, ad order share, margin, rank stability.
- Action: reduce waste but do not shut off ads blindly if they protect important traffic.

### 旺季前

- Goal: preheat data before CPC and demand spike.
- Ads: early discovery, exact setup for seasonal terms, inventory-aware budget planning.
- Metrics: search trend, competitor sales, CPC movement, inventory arrival date.
- Evidence: seasonal articles support earlier preparation, but exact lead time is category-dependent.

### 旺季中

- Goal: capture peak traffic while avoiding uncontrolled CPC inflation.
- Ads: scale proven exact/seasonal terms, keep discovery narrow, protect product pages and brand terms.
- Metrics: spend velocity, conversion, inventory, ranking, TACOS.
- Risk: late broad testing can waste peak budget.

### 淡季

- Goal: preserve useful data and rank at acceptable cost.
- Ads: keep profitable exact/defense; reduce broad and high-CPC rank pushes unless strategic.
- Metrics: demand trend, conversion, CPC, inventory.
- Confidence: low to medium, because extracted evidence is thinner than for launch and ranking cases.

### 清库存期

- Goal: turn inventory into cash while controlling avoidable loss.
- Ads: exact high-CVR terms, product targeting against weaker competitors, deal/activity support if margin allows.
- Metrics: inventory days, gross margin, ACOS tolerance, conversion, price.
- Risk: using ranking-push logic when the real goal is sell-through.

## Stage Decision Rules

- Product stage changes the interpretation of ACOS and ad order share.
- New launch can tolerate more data cost, but only with budget boundaries.
- Stable phase should not accept high ad dependency without natural-order diagnosis.
- Seasonal phase requires timing discipline; late data collection is often too slow.
- Do not assume a universal three-month Amazon traffic-support window. Treat the DOCX timeline as a launch hypothesis and verify impression share, CVR, total orders, TACOS, organic rank, inventory, and objective by marketplace/category cohort.
- Cases are comparisons, not rules. CASE001/CASE002 show ranking stalls; CASE010/CASE011 show budget and targeting pressure in a competitive category.

## Common Mistakes

- Applying stable-period ACOS rules to new launches.
- Treating seasonal products like evergreen products with unlimited testing time.
- Ignoring inventory pressure when raising bids.
- Using campaign structure before defining stage goal.
- Marking comment-only stage advice as high confidence.

## Quality Checklist

- Stage is explicitly identified.
- Primary objective is stated: data, profit, rank, defense, peak capture, or clearance.
- Budget and margin boundaries are included.
- Sample size and review window are defined.
- Natural rank and ad orders are tracked separately.
- Low-confidence stage assumptions are labeled.
